# Yellow Triangle

My project was made through a mission proposal of a challenge from the Stardence Hack Club, where I had to create my own system to run on the web. Through evaluations that I did of other projects, I understood how the creation of these projects worked. 

The basis was always a calendar and other things, but one thing that I noticed was that the design of most sites was extremely cluttered. So, through that and collecting this information, I made my own site, my web. I programmed it in a way that it would be a simple yet functional interface, and clean so that the person who is going to access it doesn't get lost. The site is simple, but well made.



It is simple, there is not much to say. It has a black background and with boxes with neon borders on the right and left. In the middle, there is a logo of a large yellow triangle and neon of bricks, and on top of it, the name, a title written **Stardence Hack Club**.

* **Left Side:** There is a timer with the options to start, stop, and pause with details in yellow. Below it, there is another box to write down like a notepad where you can write down things, and it has the option to save (and the borders are in yellow). There is also a calendar that is updated every day where the person can see the day, month, and year.
* **Right Side:** There is a box with an interesting option and the differential. In most projects, there is a calendar, timer, and search option, but in mine, there is an option of a game! It is simple: you are a square and must keep jumping over red squares to win points.
* **Center:** There is a search option where when you type anything and press search, three links from GitHub, YouTube, and Google will appear (those are the links that I decided to put).




Three languages were used:

1. **HTML:** It was used to create the structure of my code, to place its elements, texts, buttons, and boxes, and define things. It can be said that it is the main one. About the process of making it, it wasn't hard. I think that I already have quite a lot of mastery, I learned by watching classes, and I think that I reached a good and interesting result.
2. **JavaScript:** It is the brain. In it, I make the operations in the HTML, I define in it, and I make the logic. It was made by me and it was a bit time-consuming, but I actually found it interesting to program in it. Of course, I had problems at the time of programming and had to resort to resources like videos and AIs to help me understand, but it was all typed by me and my own structure.
3. **CSS:** This one gave problems! Although it is one of the easiest for me in terms of work logic, because in the choice of colors I was in doubt and I had to keep testing until finding the ideal one. CSS is very important, it is the body of my code. It gave work, but I reached a pleasant result in the colors, and also I admit I used a bit of AI to reach the correct color tone.



But in general, I liked doing the challenge and found the proposal interesting, and I think I learned more. If you need to analyze the code, it is available in the code tab of my GitHub.
